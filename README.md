# Vibes

### Your music, your vibe — a personalised YouTube Music player for Android

[Download the latest APK](https://github.com/abulimen/vibes-app/releases/latest) · [Support the project](https://myhappr.com/jonathansamuel)

</div>

> Vibes is a personal, free and open-source Android music player led by [Samuel Jonathan](https://github.com/abulimen). It brings a clean, Spotify-inspired Material 3 experience to the YouTube Music catalog, with background playback, synced lyrics, offline cache, library tools, and more.

## Features

- Stream songs, albums, playlists, and videos from YouTube Music
- Background playback, downloads, local cache, sleep timer, and skip silence
- Synced lyrics, translation, music recognition, and personalised discovery
- Library management, local playlists, and Spotify playlist import via TuneMyMusic
- Equalizer, audio normalization, tempo and pitch controls, and crossfade
- Widgets, dynamic themes, listen-together, Last.fm, and Discord Rich Presence

## About the project

Vibes is a fork of [Metrolist](https://github.com/MetrolistGroup/Metrolist). It is an independent project with credit to the original project and its contributors.

Vibes does not host or store music on its servers. Playback streams directly from YouTube Music’s catalog through your own connection. Any offline downloads or cache are stored locally on your device.
